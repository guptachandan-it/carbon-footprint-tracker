QUICK GUIDE

What should I enter?

Use your usual monthly activity values. You can enter one category or all three.

kWh

Units of electricity used, normally shown on your electricity bill.

km

Kilometres travelled by each transport mode in one month.

kg

Kilograms of household waste produced in one month.

CO₂e

A simple unit used to show estimated climate impact.

rate

The estimate used to convert your activity into kg CO₂e.
