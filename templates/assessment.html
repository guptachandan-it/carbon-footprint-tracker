What do these terms mean?

CO₂e

Carbon dioxide equivalent — one simple unit used to show the climate impact of different activities.

kg

Kilogram — the main unit used for your estimated monthly footprint.

tonne

A larger unit: 1 tonne = 1,000 kg.

emission rate

A configurable estimate used to convert your activity, such as kWh or kilometres, into kg CO₂e.
