Carbon Footprint Tracker

A web-based application designed to help users estimate and understand their personal carbon emissions.

Department of Information TechnologyAcademic Year 2026–27Theme: Sustainable Computational Solutions for Real-World Societal Challenges

PROBLEM STATEMENT

Why this project matters

Carbon emissions are produced through everyday activities such as electricity consumption, transportation, and waste generation. Many people are not aware of how much these activities contribute to their personal carbon footprint, and existing calculation methods can be difficult for regular users to understand and track.

PROJECT DEFINITION

A simple way to understand estimates

The Carbon Footprint Tracker allows users to enter basic electricity usage, transportation, and waste-generation information and obtain an estimated footprint. Results are presented through simple charts and emission breakdowns, with previous assessments available for tracking.

PROPOSED SOLUTION

One platform for the activities that matter here.

The platform brings electricity, transportation, and household-waste information together. It uses emission factors to calculate estimated emissions, groups results into categories, and presents them through charts so users can identify their major estimated sources.

PROJECT OBJECTIVES

What the tracker aims to do.

01

Calculate estimated emissions from electricity, transportation, and waste.

02

Show how different activities contribute to the total footprint.

03

Present results through simple charts and visualizations.

04

Store previous assessments for future comparison.

05

Help users identify areas where emissions can be reduced.

06

Increase awareness and encourage practical, sustainable daily habits.

CONCLUSION

Everyday data, made understandable.

By bringing electricity, transportation, and waste data together, the project converts routine activities into understandable estimated emission values. Charts and historical records make it easier to monitor changes and identify major sources of emissions.

This is an academic project. All footprint results are estimates based on configured emission factors.
